What this before-you-buy checklist helps you decide

This free checklist helps you pause before a purchase and review need, use cases, alternatives, discounts, storage, and timing. It is designed for moments when you want to avoid impulse purchases, reduce purchase regret, or decide whether waiting is smarter than buying now.

Recommended for

  • People who want to reduce impulse purchases
  • Anyone who regrets small purchases later
  • People who want a calmer buying habit

Reusable situations

Use it for daily items, clothes, gadgets, appliances, subscriptions, online shopping, or anything you are considering buying.
