What this checklist helps you decide

This checklist helps you decide whether to replace an old item, wait a little longer, or improve how you use it. It looks beyond price and considers daily inconvenience, lost time, and practical value.

Recommended for

  • People unsure about when to replace an item
  • Anyone deciding whether something is still good enough
  • People who want to organize spending on tools or daily items

How to use it

Think of the item you may replace and check the statements that apply. The result helps you choose between replacing it, waiting, or trying a no-cost improvement first.
